We connected live Google Search Console telemetry and Cloudways production server logs on ezycashforcars.com.au. Click the tabs below to explore what the data uncovered:

WHY IT MATTERS: Visitors searching for Brisbane scrap quotes land on a generic homepage, get confused, and bounce. Over 80,000 potential local leads were lost.
DATA-INFORMED FIX: SEO & WordPress Devs restructure inner links and canonical hierarchy to direct all Brisbane authority to the dedicated landing page.
Traffic Impact -64.9% Organic collapse in 7 days
Affected Money Pages 4 URLs Brisbane, Sydney Scrap, etc.
Yoast Database Flag noindex=1 In wp_postmeta table
Root Cause Discovery Instant Zero guesswork

URL: https://ezycashforcars.com.au/cash-for-cars-brisbane/ ➔ Excluded by 'noindex' tag
Database verification: SELECT * FROM wp_postmeta WHERE meta_key = '_yoast_wpseo_meta-robots-noindex'
THE VALUE: Without connecting GSC + DB, the SEO team would wait 2 weeks blaming Google core algorithm updates. With live telemetry, the exact rogue setting was corrected immediately.
